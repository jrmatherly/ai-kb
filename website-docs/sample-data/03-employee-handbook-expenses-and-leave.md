# Employee Handbook (Excerpt): Expenses and Leave

**Effective date**: 2024-01-01\
**Applies to**: all full-time NovaTech employees\
**Owning departments**: Human Resources, Finance

## 1. Travel expenses

### 1.1 Transportation

| Level | Flights | High-speed rail | Local transport |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M3 and above | Economy | First class | Actual cost, capped at 300 CNY per day |
| M1–M2 | Economy | Second class | Actual cost, capped at 200 CNY per day |
| Staff | Economy (approval 3 days ahead) | Second class | Actual cost, capped at 150 CNY per day |

**Rules**:

- Book flights through the company's designated travel platform. Tickets you buy yourself must be claimed within 7 working days after the trip ends.
- For a high-speed rail ride longer than 6 hours you can request a first-class upgrade, approved by your direct manager by email.

### 1.2 Accommodation

| City tier | Nightly cap (tax included) |
| --- | --- |
| Tier-1 cities (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen) | 600 CNY |
| New tier-1 cities | 450 CNY |
| Other cities | 350 CNY |

You pay any amount over the cap yourself, unless the event organizer books the rooms and Finance grants a special approval.

### 1.3 Meals and allowances

- Meals during business trips are **not reimbursed separately**. You receive a daily travel allowance instead:
  - Tier-1 cities in China: 150 CNY / day
  - Other cities in China: 120 CNY / day
  - Abroad: per Finance's monthly exchange rate table
- Client entertainment meals go through the **business entertainment** process, separate from travel expenses.

## 2. Everyday expenses

### 2.1 Deadline

Submit every invoice in an OA expense claim **within 30 days of the expense date**. Late claims need a written explanation signed by both your direct manager and the finance manager.

### 2.2 Common categories

| Category | Notes | Required attachments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office supplies | Unit price < 500 CNY | Invoice + purchase list |
| Training | External certifications, industry conferences | Invoice + training notice + completion certificate |
| Test equipment | Prototypes and sensors for R&D testing | Invoice + project number + asset intake form |

**R&D staff** who buy smart home hubs, sensors or other test hardware must first create an "R&D prototype" entry in the asset system, then link it to the expense claim. Use the model and specifications in *Product Manual: Smart Home Hub Pro*.

## 3. Annual leave and time off

### 3.1 Annual leave days

Based on years of service (counted from the hire date):

| Years of service | Annual leave days |
| --- | --- |
| Under 1 year | 5 days |
| 1–10 years | 10 days |
| Over 10 years | 15 days |

Employees hired before March 31 get annual leave for that year prorated by the remaining months. Employees hired after that get full annual leave from the next year.

### 3.2 Leave process

1. Submit a leave request in OA in advance and choose the type (annual / sick / personal, and so on).
2. Up to 3 days: direct manager approves. 3–7 days: department director approves. Over 7 days: filed with HR.
3. Sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days needs a certificate from a tier-2 or higher hospital.

### 3.3 Conflicts with projects

If your leave overlaps a fixed release milestone (such as the firmware 3.5 staged rollout or the knowledge base POC acceptance), announce it in the project group and name a **handover contact**. For leave related to Zhang Ming's team POC, also notify Product Director Chen Hao.

## 4. Violations

- Fake invoices or duplicate claims: once confirmed, the money is recovered and handled under the *Employee Rewards and Penalties Policy*.
- Unapproved travel abroad: expenses are not reimbursed.
